Data Guide — What do I need?
This dashboard runs a full DCF + WACC valuation. The table below shows every input, what it means, and where to find it. Monetary values go in millions USD. Most data is available free on Yahoo Finance, Macrotrends, or the company's annual report (10-K).
01 · Company Information
FieldWhat it is & where to find it
Company NameFull legal name of the company (e.g. Tesla, Inc.)
TickerStock exchange symbol — e.g. TSLA, AAPL, MSFT
Current Share Price ($)Today's share price in USD. Yahoo Finance → Quote
Shares Outstanding (M)Total diluted shares in millions. Yahoo Finance → Statistics → Shares Outstanding or 10-K cover page.
Cash & Short-term Investments ($M)Cash, equivalents, and short-term investments. Balance sheet → Cash & Cash Equivalents + Short-term Investments.
Total Debt ($M)All interest-bearing debt (short + long term). Balance sheet → Total Debt (or Short-term Debt + Long-term Debt).
Corporate Tax Rate (%)Effective tax rate. Income statement → Income Tax / Pre-tax Income. Typical 15–28%. Used across all sections.
Net Debt is calculated automatically: Total Debt − Cash & Short-term Investments. Negative = net cash position.
02 · Historical Financials (most recent full year, in M)
FieldWhat it is & where to find it
RevenueTotal net revenue / sales. Income statement → Revenue
EBITEarnings Before Interest & Tax = Operating Income. Income statement → Operating Income
D&ADepreciation & Amortization. Cash flow statement → D&A or income statement footnotes.
CapExCapital Expenditures (enter as positive). Cash flow statement → Capital expenditures
Change in NWCIncrease in net working capital (positive = cash outflow). Cash flow statement → Changes in working capital
Tax Rate (%)Effective tax rate. Income statement → Income tax / Pre-tax income. Typical range 15–28%.
03 · WACC Inputs
FieldWhat it is & where to find it
Risk-Free Rate (%)Yield on 10-year US Treasury. finance.yahoo.com → ^TNX or FRED. Typical: 4–5%.
Equity Risk Premium (%)Extra return investors demand above the risk-free rate. Use Damodaran's current US ERP. Typical: 4.5–6%.
Beta (β)Stock's sensitivity to the market (1.0 = market). Yahoo Finance → Statistics → Beta (5Y monthly).
Pre-tax Cost of Debt (%)Interest rate the company pays on its debt. Estimate: Interest Expense ÷ Total Debt, or look up the bond yield. Macrotrends → Interest Expense ÷ Total Debt.
Market Value of Equity ($M)Auto-calculated: Share Price × Shares Outstanding (M). No need to enter manually — updates when you fill in Company Information.
Market Value of Debt ($M)Book value of total debt is a common proxy. Balance sheet → Total Debt. Same as the "Total Debt" you entered above.
Formula: WACC = Ke × (E/V) + Kd×(1−t) × (D/V) · Ke = Risk-free rate + β × ERP · Kd(after-tax) = Pre-tax cost of debt × (1 − tax rate)
04 · Forecast Assumptions
FieldWhat it is & where to find it
Revenue Growth Yr 1 (%)Expected revenue growth next year. Use analyst consensus or your own estimate. Yahoo Finance → Analysis → Revenue Estimate
Revenue Growth Yr 2–5 (%)Average annual growth for years 2 through 5. Often company guidance or sector average.
EBIT Margin (%)Target operating profit margin. Use historical average or analyst estimate. Macrotrends → Operating Margin
Terminal Growth Rate (%)Perpetuity growth rate after yr 10. Should not exceed long-run GDP growth (~2–3%). Conservative default: 2.5%.
